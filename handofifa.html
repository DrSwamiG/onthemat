🜂 Hand of Ifá

4 Cowrie Shell Iyawo Lesson • SwamiG Institute

Lesson Objective

Develop the ability to ask clean questions, perform 4 cowrie casting, and align with truth through the Hand of Ifá.

SGI Pillars Integration

  • Know Thyself → Who is asking?
  • Cleanse & Purify → What is the intention?
  • Alignment with Truth → Can you accept the answer?

Orientation: Hand of Ifá

The shells do not think. The shells respond only to the question asked.

The quality of the answer = the quality of the question.

Five Response System

Open Name Response Meaning
4 Alafia YES Alignment
3 Etawa PROBABLY Adjust + Recast
2 Ejife DEFINITELY Strong confirmation
1 Okanran DOUBTFUL Reframe question
0 Oyeku NO Stop / shift direction

Hands-On Practice

Orientation Drill

Identify Open (mouth visible) vs Closed (mouth hidden).

Casting Drill

  1. Hold 4 cowries
  2. State: “I align with truth”
  3. Cast shells
  4. Record result

Question Discipline

Garbage In = Garbage Out

Well-Formed

“Should I accept the job offered today?”

Ill-Formed

“Should I accept the job or wait?”

Create:

  • 3 Well-Formed Questions
  • 3 Ill-Formed Questions

Closing Practice

Speak aloud:

“May my Ori be aligned
May my question be clean
May my ears hear truth
Even when truth is not comfortable”

21-Day Assignment

  • Cast daily
  • Record question
  • Record outcome

Consistency builds trust in Ori.

SGI Teaching

The shells are not powerful.

Alignment is powerful.
